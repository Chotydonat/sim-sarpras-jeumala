## Brand & Style

This design system blends **Luminous Clean Neumorphism** with **Origami 3D Papercraft** elegance. Tailored for modern madrasah facilities operations and institutional asset stewardship, the interface delivers pristine administrative efficiency alongside welcoming educational prestige. 

### Aesthetic Pillars
- **Luminous Atmospheric Canvas**: Crisp, pure-white floating containers (`#FFFFFF`) hover effortlessly over an ice-blue sky gradient canvas (`#F0F7FF` to `#E0F2FE`), eliminating dense, muddy grey drop-shadows in favor of cyan-tinted ambient glows.
- **Origami Geometry**: Structural planes, faceted container headers, folded navigation badges, and paper-plane-inspired dimensional accents convey disciplined structure and institutional care.
- **Tactile Pill Ergonomics**: Interactive components—buttons, filters, search bars, and status trackers—utilize full pill contours with luminous multi-layered edge definitions.
- **Tone of Voice & Emotion**: Precise, pristine, modern, authoritative yet deeply accessible. The UI instills high trust, operational accountability, and calm order under mission-critical educational workflows.

## Colors

The color palette establishes high visual hierarchy through chromatic sky blues anchored against deep slate and luminous white planes.

### Hierarchy & Functional Assignment
- **Primary Sapphire (`#0284C7`)**: The operational lifeline of the system. Governs primary interactive CTAs, tab indicators, focal metric highlights, and navigational keyframes.
- **Primary Deep (`#0369A1`)**: The interactive pressure point. Deployed on button hover states, active borders, and depth gradient endpoints.
- **Accent Royal (`#1D4ED8`)**: Specialized for high-emphasis service banners and tiered categorization containers.
- **Neutral Dark Slate (`#0F172A`)**: Primary typographic hierarchy and commanding dark-surface anchor (fixed navigation bars and official registry actions).
- **Secondary Slate (`#64748B`)**: Field labels, metadata strings, table headers, and passive asset indicators.

### Status & Third-Party Integration
- **Emergency Flare (`#EF4444` / `#FEE2E2`)**: Urgent work orders, severe physical asset damage alerts, and live incident dispatches.
- **Prestige Amber (`#F59E0B` / `#FEF3C7`)**: Maintenance in progress, technician scheduled, and operational ratings.
- **Emerald Verification (`#10B981` / `#DCFCE7`)**: Verified operational status, class readiness, and completed maintenance.
- **WhatsApp Direct (`#25D366` / `#DCF8C6` / `#075E54`)**: Dedicated modal ticket transmission preview and instant communication dispatch.

## Typography

The typography relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering geometric clarity, contemporary rounded apertures, and exceptional legibility across dense institutional data tables and large display heroes.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Display & Large Titles**: Use weight `800` paired with negative tracking (`-0.02em`) to craft an authoritative yet tech-forward presence.
- **Section Headers (`headline-lg` / `headline-md`)**: Crisp, confident headers that divide complex facility groupings, room listings, and verification workflows.
- **Data & Administrative Tables**: Column titles leverage `label-table-head` set to uppercase with wide tracking (`0.05em`) and slate coloring (`#64748B`), establishing effortless vertical scanability.
- **Operational Status Indicators**: `badge-mini` is set in bold tracking to guarantee immediate legibility inside compact folded badges and corner ribbons.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system employs an **8px base grid** inside a constrained **1240px fluid max-width container** for desktop views, ensuring balanced margins and effortless scanning.

### Layout Blueprint
- **Desktop Main Container**: Fixed maximum width of `1240px` with `margin: 0 auto`.
- **Hero & Search Widget**: Asymmetrical split (Left: 55% copy and institutional credentialing; Right: 45% interactive faceted widget or origami 3D visual cluster).
- **Navigation Shell**: Floating sticky glassmorphic pill header (Height `72px`, horizontal padding `28px`).
- **Administrative Split View**: Fixed left utility rail (`240px` width) with dynamic flexible content viewport.
- **Emergency Dispatch Multi-Pane**: Dual-column modal modal layout (55% data inputs, 45% simulated live WhatsApp transmission panel).
- **Responsive Behavior**: Transitions smoothly to a unified 1-column stack below `1024px`, activating fixed bottom pill action sheets and `52px` touch targets on mobile viewports (`390px` to `768px`).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy muddy shadows, replacing them with **Sapphire-Tinted Ambient Luminance** and crisp **Origami Inset Rim Lighting**.

### Depth Layers
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Pure tinted sky backdrop (`#F0F7FF` to `#E0F2FE`), framing elevated white geometric modules.
- **Level 1 (Floating Cards & Content Tiles)**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces with subtle sapphire dispersion:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08), 0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04);`
  Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Hero Floating Interactive Containers)**: Multi-layered dimensionality with crisp inner paper fold illumination:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 45px -10px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.18), inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85);`
- **Level 3 (Interactive Hover Elevation)**:
  `transform: translateY(-4px);`
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -4px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.20);`
- **Level 4 (Pill CTAs & Glowing Anchors)**:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.35);`
  Emergency variant: `0 8px 20px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.30);`
- **Level 5 (Modals & Command Center Overlays)**:
  `box-shadow: 0 30px 70px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.25);`
  Overlay backdrop: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.
- **Glassmorphic Floating Nav**:
  Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`.
  Border: `1px solid rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The design system pairs full **pill contours** (`9999px`) for actions and status indicators with **architectural rounded rectangles** (`16px` to `24px`) for structured cards and dialog containers.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`)**: Interactive buttons, navigation links, primary search bars, segmented filter selectors, and status pill badges.
- **Hero Containers & Modals (`24px`)**: The central interactive facility dashboard, authentication dialogs, and high-impact hero widgets.
- **Catalog & Room Cards (`16px`)**: Standard inventory modules, classroom cards, and floating statistic tiles.
- **Internal Elements (`12px`)**: Form inputs, select menus, dropzones, and messaging chat bubbles.
- **Origami Cuts & Accents**: 45-degree corner badge folds (`100% Siap KBM`) and crisp linear divider treatments reflect folded physical paper sheets.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Sapphire Pill Button**: High-chroma `#0284C7` background transitioning to `#0369A1` on hover. High-contrast white typography, `border-radius: 9999px`, padding `12px 28px`, supported by an ambient sapphire glow (`0 8px 20px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.35)`). Active transform `translateY(1px)`.
- **Secondary Ghost Pill Button**: `#FFFFFF` background with `1.5px solid #0284C7` border and text `#0284C7`. On hover: `background: #E8F2FE`.
- **Emergency Alert Pill Button**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `2px solid #EF4444` border, `#EF4444` text, accompanied by pulsing indicator radar icon. Direct submit variant uses solid `#EF4444` with white text and `0 8px 20px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.30)`.
- **WhatsApp Direct Action Button**: High-conversion `linear-gradient(135deg, #25D366, #128C7E)`, pure white text, subtle animated shimmer sweep, and WhatsApp chat icon.

### Form Inputs & Dropdowns
- **Height & Bounds**: `48px` standard height, `border-radius: 12px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Typography & Placeholder**: Font size `14px`, text color `#0F172A`, placeholder `#64748B`.
- **Active Focus State**: Border `#0284C7`, ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`, smooth transition `0.2s ease`.
- **Upload Dropzone**: Dashed perimeter `2px dashed #38BDF8`, soft blue surface `#F0F7FF`, with centered origami camera/cloud iconography.

### Status Pills & Chips
- **Baik / Ready (`#10B981`)**: Background `#DCFCE7`, text `#166534`, `border-radius: 9999px`, padding `4px 12px`.
- **Proses / Scheduled (`#0284C7`)**: Background `#DBEAFE`, text `#0369A1`, padding `4px 12px`.
- **Menunggu Teknisi (`#F59E0B`)**: Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, padding `4px 12px`.
- **Rusak Berat (`#EF4444`)**: Background `#FEE2E2`, text `#991B1B`, padding `4px 12px`.

### Cards & Container Panels
- **Classroom & Facility Cards**: Constructed with `#FFFFFF`, `border-radius: 16px`, `border: 1px solid #E2E8F0`, and ambient drop-shadow. Hover triggers `translateY(-4px)` with sapphire shadow amplification and circular action button highlight (`36px` diameter).
- **Origami Metric Widgets**: Dual-tone deep navy background (`linear-gradient(135deg, #0B192C, #0F2847)`), featuring high-contrast white metrics, electric sky `#38BDF8` sub-labels, and subtle translucent origami facet watermarks.

### Specialized Incident & WhatsApp Preview Modal
